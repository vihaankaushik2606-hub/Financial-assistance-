Know what you’re choosing.

What do the AI allowances mean?

At launch, daily AI prompt allowances are planned as 15 for Lab Starter, 40 for Research Workspace and 100 for Lab Pro. The annual pass uses the same daily allowance. Estimated daily token budgets in the launch model are approximately 45,000, 160,000 and 500,000 respectively. Tokens measure AI input and output; these estimates are not extra prompts or a guaranteed response length. Paid quotas are not currently active.

How will annual billing work?

The proposed annual passes are ₹600, ₹1,200 and ₹2,000, billed for a full year. Free Sandbox remains ₹0. Before subscriptions open, the final checkout will state the total, any applicable taxes, cancellation and refund terms. No payment is taken on this page.

What about market-data coverage?

History length, supported companies, exchange delays and quarterly availability depend on the licensed data provider and its coverage. Missing figures are labelled as unavailable. Financial Modeling Prep supplies market data; this does not imply endorsement or an exclusive partnership.

Does a plan include investment advice?

These are software and financial-education plans. AI is instructed to decline investment recommendations, trade instructions and guaranteed-return claims. Scenarios use your assumptions; they are not price predictions or promises. Plan names do not represent SEBI registration, professional certification or regulatory approval.
