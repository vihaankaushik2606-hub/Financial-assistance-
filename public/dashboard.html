How this analysis works

Calculations use annual statements for one company, reporting date and currency. Growth compares the closest available prior annual period, 300–430 days earlier. Missing values stay missing. Explanations follow the figures and do not use AI. Ratios need industry context; read the annual report notes before drawing conclusions.
