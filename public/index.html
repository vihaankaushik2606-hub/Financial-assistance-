03 / Manual analysis (optional fallback)

Company Truth Scanner

Rule-based · not AI

Enter figures from a reliable filing. Use the same currency and matching reporting periods. The app will calculate ratios and identify patterns to investigate, not assert fraud or predict outcomes.

Enter figures and run the scanner to see calculated ratios and research prompts.

Source status: figures have not been entered. Any manual figures remain unverified until you check the cited filing.

04 / AI assistant

Ask ChatGPT about your analysis

AI-assisted · verify answers

Ask for explanations of financial terms, ratios, or warning patterns. You can optionally include your scanner results. Do not enter passwords, API keys, or private information.

AI assistant needs an OPENAI_API_KEY configured securely on the server.

04 / Scenario lab

Test assumptions, not predictions

Illustrative only

Scenarios calculate from your current-period revenue and assumed growth/margin. They are not target prices and are not forecasts unless independently justified.

Educational research tool only. Not investment advice. Provider data may be delayed, incomplete, or subject to usage limits. Confirm important figures against official company filings and exchange disclosures.
