How this calculator works

Revenue = base revenue × volume factor × price factor. Variable costs = base variable costs × volume factor × unit-cost factor. Fixed costs change only by the entered fixed-cost percentage. Interest = average debt × adjusted annual rate. Profit before tax = revenue − variable costs − fixed costs − interest.

Break-even revenue = (fixed costs + interest) ÷ contribution margin, assuming the same price and variable-cost mix. A two-percentage-point rate increase changes 10% to 12%, not 10.2%.

Assumptions are independent changes from the current situation; the alternative does not build on the first scenario. No probability is assigned. The model excludes taxes, principal repayments, depreciation unless included in your costs, working-capital needs and cash-flow timing. It does not model how price changes affect customer demand. Profit is not cash flow.

Educational hypothetical modelling, without company valuations, price targets or investment recommendations. Calculations run in your browser and do not use market-data or AI calls. Inputs are not automatically saved.
