Financial warning-sign checklist

Review patterns use transparent screening thresholds, not regulatory or industry standards. “Pattern not triggered” does not mean healthy or safe. Missing data is never a pass. These checks cannot establish fraud.

Method and limits

Checks use consecutive provider-standardized quarters with matching symbols, dates and currencies. Cash flow must lag positive net income for all three quarters. Leverage review requires a debt-to-equity increase of at least 0.5×; margin review requires a fall of at least 5 percentage points. Negative equity, missing fields and gaps produce Insufficient data. Quarterly cash flow can require adjustment from year-to-date figures in original filings; verify the provider’s basis. Seasonal and exceptional items may explain a flag. Financial firms need specialist analysis.
